# Y-sort check: the player in front of and behind furniture, on the
# "furniture_room" fixture (seed 1). Spawn (122,72); tables on (122-123,70)
# and (124-125,72); a side-on cupboard on (117,71).
# Presentation scenario: compare scene.png against the old fixed layers,
# where every piece of furniture drew over the player. Collision keeps the
# overlaps to a few pixels, so only in_front_of_table differs from them.
seed 1
window 1280 720
scale 1.5

checkpoint spawn

hold W 30                     # up against the front edge of the (122,70) table
checkpoint in_front_of_table  # player is lower: its hat now covers the table leg

hold D 40                     # along row 71, over the (124,72) table
checkpoint behind_table       # player is higher: the table hides its feet

hold A 140                    # left until the cupboard on (117,71) stops us
checkpoint beside_cupboard

hold S 8                      # feet drop below the cupboard's base
checkpoint below_cupboard
end
